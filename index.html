How to add one in Gmail

  1. Open this page from its GitHub Pages link (not the downloaded file), so the icons copy across with proper web links.
  2. Click and drag to highlight just your signature in its white box, then press Ctrl+C.
  3. In Gmail, go to Settings (cog) > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new.
  4. Click into the signature box and press Ctrl+V. Don't paste the code, paste the copied signature.
  5. Under "Signature defaults", pick it for new emails and replies, then scroll down and click Save Changes.
